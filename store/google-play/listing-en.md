# Google Play listing — EN

**Name (max 30):** Werkit

**Short description (max 80):**
Field ops and MRO: work orders, two warehouses, and GPS during a work session.

**Full description:**

Werkit is for field crews: dispatchers plan work orders, workers complete them on the phone.

• Work and repair (MRO) orders with priorities
• One material per work order; spare-part list for repairs
• Route GPS only during an active, non-stationary session — not 24/7 fleet tracking
• Photos and notes from the job site
• Reminders for upcoming and overdue orders
• Password sign-in, optional on-device biometrics

The app requires an account issued by the employer (organization). It is not a consumer tracker or messenger.

Privacy policy: https://werkit.cncsolutions.dev/privacy-policy
